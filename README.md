# Bubu & Dudu Day (with Lani)

A tiny static surprise for Crystal. Soft kawaii sticker-style Bubu and Dudu (SVG), plus their chubby grey British Shorthair cat Lani.

## Who is who
- **Dudu** (brown bear) = **Maksym** (husband)
- **Bubu** (white bear, black ears, dark bow) = **Crystal** (wife)
- **Baby boy Nikola** = small brown bear like mini Dudu / Maksym
- **Lani** = their chubby grey British Shorthair

## How to open

1. Open `index.html` in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).
2. Double-click the file, or drag it into a browser tab.
3. No install, no server, no internet needed after the file is on your device.

On a phone: copy `index.html` to the device and open it in the mobile browser, or host the folder briefly on a local network.

## How to play

### Interactive
1. Toggle **Bubu (Crystal)** or **Dudu (Maksym)** (who you control). The leader holds items and speaks first.
2. Tap an activity:
   - **Coffee** - walk in, sit, steaming cups, croissant nibble, crumbs and sparkles.
   - **Adventure** - walk cycle, bouncing suitcase, fluttering map, drifting clouds, happy hops.
   - **Bedroom** - curtains draw, lights dim, floating hearts, soft blush, then baby boy Nikola (mini brown bear) with a focus zoom. Wholesome meme tone only.
3. Lani wanders or sits nearby in each scene (walk and tail flick).
4. Use **Sound on / Sound off** for soft built-in SFX (stays muted if you prefer quiet).

### Play story
Tap **Play story** for a scripted sequence:
- Coffee: 10 seconds
- Adventure: 10 seconds
- Bedroom to baby boy: 15 seconds (baby focus near the end)

When it finishes, use **Done / Replay** or play interactively again.


## Sound
Soft synthesized cues via the Web Audio API (no audio files or CDN). Mute or unmute with **Sound on / Sound off**; the choice is saved in localStorage. Audio unlocks on your first tap (browser policy).

## Art notes
Characters are inline SVG recreations of the classic Bubu/Dudu sticker look: flat fills (no heavy shading), thick espresso outlines (~#3E2723), large round heads, pear bodies, stubby limbs, w-mouth with tiny tongue, and big circular blushes (orange-yellow on Dudu, soft pink on Bubu). Idle default is holding hands close together on a pastel pink backdrop.
